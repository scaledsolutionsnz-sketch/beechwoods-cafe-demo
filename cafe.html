Welcome

Welcome to Beechwoods Café

With ample parking for cars, caravans, campervans, boats, buses and trucks, stopping is easy. Stretch your legs, let the kids enjoy the playground, walk the dog on our grassed area, or simply settle in beside the fire with a great coffee.

Wherever you're headed, we're worth the stop — generous country food, barista coffee and genuine hospitality, seven days a week.

Why stop here

The best stop between destinations

Beechwoods Café sits on the main route between Nelson, the West Coast and Christchurch, making it an ideal place to pause, refuel and recharge. There's plenty of room to park caravans, campervans, trailers, buses and trucks.

Our Story

Hospitality comes naturally

Beechwoods Café is proudly independent and proudly part of the Murchison community. Our focus is simple: serve fresh food, make great coffee, offer genuine hospitality and create a place people want to return to. Whether you're a local or just passing through, you're always welcome.
