Welcome in

A warm room in the middle of the mountains.

Set in the heart of Murchison, Beechwoods is the kind of place where the coffee is good, the food is hearty, and nobody minds a carload of kids or muddy boots. Settle in beside the fire, or spread out under the trees.

Whether you have five minutes or an hour, you will get the same thing every time: generous country food and genuine hospitality, made fresh.

Wherever you're headed, we're worth the stop.
Food & coffee
