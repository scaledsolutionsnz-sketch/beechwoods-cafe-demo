Our kitchen, on the road

The cafe you love, catering your celebration

Beechwoods Catering and Weddings is the events arm of the Beechwoods kitchen in Murchison. It is the same hands, the same care and the same generous country cooking, brought out to your venue for the days that really count.

We keep it personal. You talk to the people who will actually cook and serve your food, and we shape a menu that fits your celebration rather than squeezing you into a set package.

  • Weddings and events across Murchison, Nelson, Tasman and the Buller
  • Menus tailored to your day, your numbers and your budget
  • Generous, seasonal food using produce from the top of the South
